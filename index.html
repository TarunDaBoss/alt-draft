How it works

  1. You drop in an image.

    It opens on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

  2. An AI in your browser writes a first draft.

    The model is LFM2.5-VL by Liquid AI, run with Transformers.js on your own graphics chip. The first visit downloads it (about 550 MB); your browser keeps a copy for next time.

  3. You finish it.

    The draft is a starting point, not finished alt text. It's a small model: it can misname things, misread text or miss the point of the image. You know why the image is there, so you make the call.

Good alt text is short, says what matters for the page it's on, and quotes any important text in the image. If an image is only decoration, mark it decorative and leave the alt empty.

Alt Draft needs a browser with WebGPU: a recent Chrome or Edge, Safari 26 or later, or Firefox 141 or later on Windows.

Privacy

Your image never leaves your device. The AI runs inside this browser tab, so there is no server to send it to.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies.
  • What gets downloaded: this page (from GitHub Pages), the Transformers.js library (from jsDelivr) and the AI model files (from Hugging Face). Like any website, those services see an ordinary request for files. They never see your image, your draft or what you type.
  • What's saved on your device: a copy of the AI model, so you don't download it twice, and a small note that it's ready. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes both.
  • Your clipboard is only written to when you press “Approve & Copy”.
